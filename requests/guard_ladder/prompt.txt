[ORIGINAL PROMPT] (paste here if you have it)

IDENTITY (attach identity/reference.png and identity/approved_poses.png):
  The same character as the attached references: Grumpy mustached guard: silver armour and helmet, blue sash/tabard, brown belt and gloves, sheathed sword.
  Match the references exactly: face, hair, hood / helmet, outfit, colours, proportions, line weight and shading.
  Do not restyle, simplify, chibify, recolour or add accessories. Every frame is the same character at the same scale.
  His identity reference is concept art whose tabard differs from every sprite: match the approved poses first.

LAYOUT:
  One sprite sheet, 8 frames in a single row (or a regular grid), each in its own equal cell, transparent or flat plain background.
  Full body in every frame, nothing cropped, side view facing right (back view for the ladder).
  The same ground line (feet level) and the same figure height in every frame; no text, no frame numbers, no shadows that touch the next cell.

MOTION:
  An 8-drawing back-view ladder cycle that climbs two rungs: diagonal hand and foot move together, then swap.
  Draw 8 frames, back view, on 640x576 cells. Rungs every 67 px. In the first half the right hand and left foot each move up one rung while the left hand and right foot grip (filled dots move DOWN 16.8 px per frame in the cell as he rises); then swap. Planted dots = gripping, rings = moving. The loop rises 135 px per cycle (two rungs).
  Frames:
    1 right hand + left foot move up (1/4)
    2 right hand + left foot move up (2/4)
    3 right hand + left foot move up (3/4)
    4 right hand + left foot move up (4/4)
    5 left hand + right foot move up (1/4)
    6 left hand + right foot move up (2/4)
    7 left hand + right foot move up (3/4)
    8 left hand + right foot move up (4/4)
  Each frame must be a different drawing: no repeated frames, no near-copies (timing and holds are set later in the engine).
  Motion should change about the same amount between neighbouring frames.

AVOID (what went wrong before, from the handoff and the owner's feedback):
  frames that repeat the same pose; feet that slide or float; a passing pose that is missing; the look changing between
  frames or from the other actions (hood shape, hair, colours, thinness); reaching into the air or pushing off nothing.
