Drag a prop to move it; drag its top edge to change its height, its left/right edge to resize (patrols and trigger zones: drag an end). Lines are transitions for the chosen character, judged from its drawings: green = it can, amber = marginal, red dashed = it can't. "Play from here" opens the level runner at the last place you clicked, with this (unsaved) level. Click a problem to select what it is about.
